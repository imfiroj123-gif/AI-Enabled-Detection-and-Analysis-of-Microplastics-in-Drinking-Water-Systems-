Spectroscopic Verification Guide

Optical microscopy and computer vision provide rapid candidate screening. Chemical confirmation of synthetic polymers requires targeted laser micro-spectroscopy:

Micro-Raman Spectroscopy:
Optimal for particles < 20 µm. Non-destructive, analyzes single-particle vibrational fingerprints.
Micro-FTIR (ATR/Transmission):
Standard for particles > 20 µm. Distinguishes Polyethylene (PE), Polypropylene (PP), and PET from natural cellulose.
Important Regulatory Note: The presence of detected candidate particles does not automatically classify drinking water as hazardous or in violation of potable water regulations, as formal health-based guideline values for microplastics have not yet been codified by health agencies.
